# Road Dog — Brand guide (v1.0.0)

## Identity
- Product: **Road Dog**. Companion: **Sam (Sam(wise))**, short "Sam".
- Taglines: "Every road is better with a Road Dog." (primary),
  "Your AI. Your crew. Your road." (secondary).
- Voice: modern urban industrial — confident, dry-witted, loyal, plain about
  limits, no exaggerated slang, no stereotypes.

## Visual system
- **Palette:** graphite black `#17171B` (badge ground), charcoal `#1F1F26`
  (face panel), electric purple `#7C3AED` / `#8B5CF6` / `#A78BFA` (signal),
  off-white ink `#ECECF1` / `#F4F4F7`. Terminal/installer gradient:
  `#A78BFA → #8B5CF6 → #7C3AED` (xterm-256: 141/99/93).
- **UI tokens:** the signal hue lives in `--accent`, `--ring`, `--info`,
  `--chat-bubble-out`, `--viz-trigger` (light: `262 83% 58%`; dark:
  `258 90% 70%`) in `jarvis/ui/web/frontend/src/index.css`. Terminal themes
  mirror it (`terminalThemes.ts`).
- **Logo:** `assets/brand/road-dog-logo.svg` (badge + wordmark),
  `assets/brand/road-dog-mark.svg` (icon source). Icons are generated by
  `/scripts/road_dog_icons.py` into `assets/icons/` (ICO/ICNS/PNG) and the
  frontend `public/` — historical filenames kept so builds stay wired.
- **Themes:** dark (product default), light, and a true high-contrast
  accessibility ladder (`.high-contrast`, persisted as `[ui] high_contrast`).

## Typography
- UI: Inter Variable (upstream stack, unchanged) — clean, neutral, industrial.
- Installer/terminal: system monospace with ANSI-256 fallbacks.
- Wordmark in artwork: bold geometric sans (DejaVu Sans Bold in the SVG).

## Rules for contributors
- Never hardcode a colour outside the token blocks (both themes must pass).
- Every user-facing surface says Road Dog / Sam — the `jarvis` internal
  namespace, `jarvis.toml` and `jarvis` CLI are the documented exceptions.
- Keep the purple gradient continuous across a whole surface; one accent,
  never two competing hues.
